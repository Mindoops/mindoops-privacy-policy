Privacy Policy for Paper Escape

Developer: MindOops Studio

Package name: com.mindoops.paperescape

Effective date: August 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MindOops Studio handles information in connection with Paper Escape, a single-player puzzle game for Android.

1. Information We Collect

Paper Escape does not ask players to create an account, log in, submit forms, or directly provide personal information inside the game.

The game does not ask players to provide names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, payment information, contacts, photos, videos, audio recordings, files, or user-generated content.

The current Android version of Paper Escape does not include advertising SDKs, social login SDKs, or third-party account services.

2. App Permissions and Device Information

Paper Escape may include standard Android network permissions, such as internet access and network-state access, to support normal app and platform functionality.

3. Platform and Store Services

When you install, update, or use Paper Escape through Google Play or an Android device, Google, Android, Unity, device manufacturers, or operating-system services may process technical information under their own terms and privacy policies.

4. Advertising, Analytics, and Third Parties

Paper Escape does not show third-party ads in the current Android version.

Paper Escape does not use third-party advertising identifiers for ad targeting, ad measurement, profiling, or marketing in the current Android version.

Paper Escape does not share personal information with third-party advertisers, ad networks, social networks, or data brokers.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Paper Escape does not create player accounts and does not maintain a server-side player account database.

Game progress and settings may be stored locally on your device. You can remove locally stored game data by clearing app storage/data for Paper Escape or uninstalling the game.

6. Children's Privacy

Paper Escape is intended for a general audience. MindOops Studio does not knowingly ask children to provide personal information through the game.

7. Security

MindOops Studio takes reasonable steps to protect information associated with Paper Escape, but no app, device, network, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

MindOops Studio may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If material changes are made, the updated policy will be posted with a new effective date.
