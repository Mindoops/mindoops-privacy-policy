This Privacy Policy explains how MindOops Studio ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with Turns. It covers the game, support requests, and versions of the game distributed with Google AdMob advertising. Advertising-related processing described below applies when an advertising-enabled version requests or displays ads.

Privacy overview
  • Playing Turns does not require a player account or login.
  • Game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • Ad-supported versions use Google AdMob. Advertising services can process device identifiers, IP addresses, interaction information, and diagnostics.
  • You can manage available advertising choices and device permissions, delete local game data, or contact us about privacy.

1. Information you provide

Turns does not require you to enter your name, email address, phone number, or payment details to play. The game does not ask you to upload contacts, photographs, videos, audio recordings, or other personal files.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, the contents of your message, and any information or attachments you choose to send. Please include only information needed to explain your request.

2. Information stored on your device

Turns stores information such as level progress, completed puzzles, coins, hints, achievements, streaks, and gameplay preferences locally. Preferences can include language, sound, music, vibration, and notification settings.

This local information lets the game remember your progress and choices. MindOops Studio does not operate a player account or cloud-save database for this version of Turns. Device or operating-system backup services may retain or restore app data according to your device and backup settings.

When an advertising service is active, information can be processed when an ad is requested, loaded, displayed, or interacted with. This processing is not limited to occasions when you click an ad.

4. Personalized advertising and privacy choices

Ads may be personalized where allowed by applicable law, your eligibility, and your choices. Other ads may be based on contextual information rather than an interest profile.

Choosing non-personalized advertising does not necessarily stop all processing of technical information. Information may still be used for functions such as ad delivery, reporting, security, and fraud prevention.

Where an advertising-enabled release presents a consent message or privacy options, you can use those controls to review the choices available to you. Consent-dependent processing is subject to the applicable consent choice. You can also contact us for help with a privacy request.

Additional controls may be available in Android's privacy or advertising settings, including resetting or deleting your advertising ID, and in Google My Ad Center. Available controls vary by device, Android version, region, and account.

5. Permissions and notifications

Turns uses internet and network-state access for online functionality such as advertising in advertising-enabled releases. The game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or precise GPS location.

Turns can schedule local gameplay reminders. On Android versions that require notification permission, the app requests that permission before showing notifications. You can manage reminders through the game's notification setting or Android's app notification settings. Reminder scheduling does not require a player account or a developer-run remote push-notification service.

Vibration and device restart information may be used to provide haptic feedback and restore local reminder schedules. Advertising-enabled builds may also declare Android's advertising ID permission, depending on the SDK and build configuration.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis for processing, the basis depends on the activity: providing a service you request, our legitimate interests in supporting and securing the game where permitted, meeting legal obligations, or consent where required. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information; restrict or object to certain processing; withdraw consent; or opt out of certain advertising uses. You may also have a right to complain to a data protection authority.
