Privacy Policy for Paper Escape

Developer: MindOops Studio

Effective date: July 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MindOops Studio handles information in connection with Paper Escape.

1. Information We Collect

Paper Escape does not ask players to create an account, log in, submit forms, or directly provide personal information inside the game.

The game does not ask players to provide names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, payment information, contacts, or user-generated content.

However, Paper Escape and the third-party services used in the game may collect or process limited technical, device, usage, analytics, advertising, and diagnostic information. This may include:

  • device and app identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID where available
  • device type, operating system, app version, language, country, or approximate location derived from network information
  • gameplay and session events, such as level started, level finished, win/loss result, playtime, and feature usage
  • advertising events, such as rewarded ad requests, ad views, ad clicks, and ad performance data
  • diagnostic, crash, performance, and stability information
  • IP address and network-related information processed by service providers to operate their services

2. How We Use Information

MindOops Studio and service providers may use information for the following purposes:

  • to operate and maintain the game
  • to provide rewarded ads, such as ads used for hints, revive, or continuing after failure
  • to understand gameplay performance, level difficulty, and player progression
  • to measure advertising performance and campaign effectiveness
  • to improve gameplay, stability, and user experience
  • to detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, security issues, or technical problems
  • to comply with legal, platform, and store requirements

3. Ads, Analytics, and Third-Party Services

Paper Escape uses third-party SDKs and services for analytics, advertising, attribution, platform features, privacy/settings tools, and game configuration. These services may collect and process information according to their own privacy policies.

The services used in Paper Escape may include:

  • YsoCorp GameUtils / YConfig for game analytics, configuration, privacy/settings tools, and publishing support.
  • AppLovin MAX for rewarded advertising and ad mediation.
  • Tenjin for attribution, analytics, and campaign measurement.
  • Meta / Facebook SDK for app events, app activation, analytics, and campaign-related measurement.
  • Google Play services and Android platform services for platform, store, security, diagnostics, and related app functionality.

These providers may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, usage events, ad interaction data, diagnostic data, and other technical information to provide their services.

4. Advertising

Paper Escape may show rewarded ads. Rewarded ads are optional ads that players can choose to watch in exchange for in-game benefits, such as hints, revive, or continuing after failure.

Advertising partners may use technical information and advertising identifiers to load ads, limit repeated ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and provide advertising services. Depending on your device settings, region, and applicable law, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

You can manage advertising choices from your device settings, including resetting or deleting your Android Advertising ID where supported. Some privacy options may also be available through the in-game settings or data privacy screen.

5. Sharing of Information

MindOops Studio does not sell personal information directly.

Information may be shared with or processed by third-party service providers used for analytics, advertising, attribution, diagnostics, hosting, security, platform services, and game operation. These providers process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

In some regions, the use of advertising or analytics SDKs may be considered a “sharing” of personal information or processing for advertising purposes under applicable privacy laws.

6. Data Retention

Paper Escape does not maintain a player account database containing personal account records.

Analytics, advertising, attribution, diagnostic, and technical information may be retained by MindOops Studio or third-party service providers only as long as needed for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, or as required by law, platform rules, security, fraud prevention, analytics, reporting, and legitimate business needs.

7. Data Security

MindOops Studio aims to take reasonable steps to protect the game and information associated with its operation. Third-party providers may also use their own security measures. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Paper Escape is intended for a general audience and is not directed specifically to children under 13. MindOops Studio does not knowingly collect names, contact details, precise location, payment information, or other directly provided personal information from children through the game.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to MindOops Studio through the game, please contact us so we can review the situation.

9. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

You may be able to limit or control certain data collection and advertising features through your device settings, Google Play settings, or in-game settings where available.

Depending on your region, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or limitation of certain information. You can contact MindOops Studio to ask questions about privacy or request review/deletion of information that MindOops Studio can reasonably identify and control.

Some information may be controlled by third-party service providers and subject to their own privacy processes.

10. International Processing

Information may be processed in countries other than your country of residence. Data protection laws may differ from country to country. Where required, service providers are expected to use appropriate safeguards for international processing.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

MindOops Studio may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the game evolves. If material changes are made, the updated policy will be posted with a new effective date.
