Privacy Policy for Turns

Developer: MindOops Studio
Package name: com.mindoops.turns
Effective date: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MindOops Studio handles information in connection with Turns, a puzzle game for Android.

1. Information We Collect Directly

Turns does not require players to create an account, log in, submit forms, or directly provide personal information inside the game.

The game does not ask players to provide:

  • names
  • email addresses
  • phone numbers
  • precise location data
  • payment information
  • contacts
  • photos, videos, audio recordings, or files
  • user-generated content

2. Local Game Data

Turns may store game progress, level status, settings, hints, preferences, and similar gameplay information locally on your device. This information is used to let the game remember your progress and settings.

You can remove locally stored game data by clearing the app storage/data in your Android device settings or by uninstalling the game.

3. App Permissions and Device Information

Turns may include standard Android permissions, such as internet access and network-state access. These permissions support normal app functionality, platform services, analytics, diagnostics, publishing SDKs, privacy/consent tools, and optional advertising-related SDK components.

MindOops Studio does not use these permissions to collect names, contact details, precise location, payment information, contacts, photos, videos, audio recordings, files, or user-generated content.

4. Third-Party SDKs and Services

Turns may include third-party SDKs and platform services for game publishing, app performance, diagnostics, analytics, attribution, privacy/consent management, and advertising infrastructure. These services may process technical information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers where available, app interactions, approximate location inferred from IP address, diagnostics, crash data, performance data, and other technical information.

Third-party services that may be connected to the game include:

  • Google Play and Android services
  • Unity services and Unity engine technology
  • GameUtils / YSO Corp publishing SDK services
  • AppLovin MAX advertising and mediation services
  • Tenjin analytics or attribution services
  • Meta / Facebook SDK services, if enabled for publishing or analytics setup

These third parties process information according to their own privacy policies. You can review some of their policies here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Unity Privacy Policy
  • AppLovin Legal and Privacy Information
  • Tenjin Privacy Policy
  • Meta Privacy Policy

5. Advertising

The current version of Turns does not require players to watch an advertisement in order to receive hints. Hint rewards may be provided through gameplay or an in-game timer.

The app may still include advertising SDK components as part of the publishing setup. If ads are enabled in this version or a future version, third-party advertising partners may process technical information such as advertising identifiers, device information, app activity, approximate location, diagnostics, and ad interaction data to deliver, limit, measure, and improve ads.

6. Analytics, Diagnostics, and Attribution

Turns may use analytics, diagnostics, and attribution services to understand game performance, crashes, gameplay progress, install sources, and general usage patterns. This helps MindOops Studio improve the game, fix bugs, and support publishing requirements.

Analytics and diagnostics information is generally technical and does not require players to directly provide their name, email address, phone number, or similar contact details.

7. Privacy and Consent Tools

Turns may include a privacy or data privacy option in the settings screen, such as a YSO Data Privacy window or similar consent/debug/privacy tool provided by the publishing SDK. This may help players review privacy-related options where required.

8. How Information Is Used

Information processed through the game or its SDKs may be used to:

  • operate the game
  • save local progress and settings
  • improve gameplay and performance
  • detect and fix crashes or bugs
  • measure installs, sessions, and level progress
  • support publishing, analytics, attribution, and compliance requirements
  • deliver, measure, or limit ads if advertising is enabled
  • prevent fraud, abuse, or security issues

9. Sharing of Information

MindOops Studio does not sell names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos, videos, audio recordings, files, or user-generated content collected directly through Turns.

Technical information may be processed by third-party SDK providers and platform services listed above for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy and in their own privacy policies.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Turns does not create player accounts and does not maintain a server-side player account database controlled directly by MindOops Studio.

Local game data can be deleted by clearing the app storage/data in your Android device settings or by uninstalling the game.

If third-party SDKs process technical information, their retention and deletion practices are governed by their own policies. You may contact MindOops Studio for privacy-related requests, and we will help where reasonably possible.

11. Children's Privacy

Turns is intended for a general audience. MindOops Studio does not knowingly ask children to provide names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, payment information, contacts, photos, videos, audio recordings, files, or other directly provided personal information through the game.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to MindOops Studio through the game, please contact us so we can review and address the request.

12. International Processing

Third-party SDK providers and platform services may process technical information on servers located in different countries. By using Turns, you understand that information may be processed outside your country of residence according to applicable laws and the privacy policies of those providers.

13. Your Choices

Depending on your device, region, and Android settings, you may be able to limit ad tracking, reset your advertising ID, manage app permissions, clear app data, or uninstall the game.

You may also use privacy or consent options shown inside the game settings, if available.

14. Security

MindOops Studio takes reasonable steps to protect information associated with the operation and support of Turns. However, no app, device, network, SDK, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

MindOops Studio may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as Turns changes. If material changes are made, the updated policy will be posted with a new effective date.
