Privacy Policy for Paper Escape

Developer: MindOops Studio

Effective date: June 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MindOops Studio handles information in connection with Paper Escape.

1. Information We Collect

Paper Escape does not ask players to create an account, log in, submit forms, or directly provide personal information inside the game.

The game does not ask players to provide names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, payment information, contacts, or user-generated content.

However, Paper Escape may collect limited technical identifiers, such as device or app-related IDs, for app functionality, analytics, diagnostics, crash investigation, performance, and game stability.

2. Device, Platform, and Store Information

When you install or access the game through a platform such as Google Play, the platform provider, your device operating system, Unity, Google Play services, or related infrastructure may process technical or diagnostic information under their own terms and privacy policies.

This may include limited device or app identifiers used to support app functionality, analytics, diagnostics, crash investigation, performance, and game stability.

3. Analytics, Ads, and Third-Party Services

Paper Escape may use platform, engine, or store-related services that process limited technical information such as device or app identifiers for app functionality, analytics, diagnostics, crash investigation, performance, and stability.

Paper Escape does not include social login or account-based services in the current version. Paper Escape does not use this data for advertising or marketing.

4. How We Use Information

MindOops Studio does not use personal information for marketing, profiling, or sale. Limited technical information may be used for:

  • supporting app functionality
  • improving gameplay and performance
  • fixing crashes or bugs
  • understanding feature quality and game stability
  • maintaining the security and integrity of the game

5. Sharing of Information

MindOops Studio does not sell personal information from Paper Escape.

Limited technical information may be processed by platform, engine, or store-related services used to operate, improve, diagnose, and maintain the game. This information is not used to personally identify players.

6. Children's Privacy

Paper Escape is intended for a general audience. MindOops Studio does not knowingly collect names, contact details, precise location, payment information, or other directly provided personal information from children through the game.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to MindOops Studio through the game, please contact us so we can review the situation.

7. Data Retention

Paper Escape does not maintain a player account database containing personal account records.

Limited technical information may be retained by platform, engine, or store-related services according to their own retention practices and only as needed for app functionality, analytics, diagnostics, performance, and stability.

8. Security

MindOops Studio aims to take reasonable steps to protect the game and any information associated with its operation. However, no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

MindOops Studio may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the game evolves. If material changes are made, the updated policy will be posted with a new effective date.
