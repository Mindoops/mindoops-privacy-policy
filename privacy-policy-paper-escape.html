Privacy Policy for Paper Escape

Developer: MindOops Studio
Effective date: May 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MindOops Studio handles information in connection with Paper Escape (the "Game"). Paper Escape is currently a single-player puzzle game being prepared for testing and release.

1. Information We Collect

Based on the current build of Paper Escape, MindOops Studio does not ask players to create an account, log in, submit forms, or directly provide personal information inside the Game.

At the current stage, the Game is designed so that MindOops Studio does not intentionally collect:

  • names
  • email addresses
  • phone numbers
  • precise location data
  • payment information
  • contacts or user-generated content

2. Device, Platform, and Store Information

When you install or access the Game through a platform such as Google Play, the platform provider, your device operating system, or related infrastructure may collect technical or diagnostic information under their own terms and privacy policies. That information is not collected directly by MindOops Studio through the Game itself.

3. Analytics, Ads, and Third-Party Services

In the current version reviewed for testing, MindOops Studio is not intentionally including third-party advertising, social login, or external analytics SDKs inside the Game.

If MindOops Studio adds features such as analytics, crash reporting, advertisements, cloud save, or account-based services in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those changes are released.

4. How We Use Information

Because the current build does not intentionally collect personal information from players inside the Game, MindOops Studio does not use personal information for marketing, profiling, or sale.

If future versions include analytics or technical diagnostics, such information would only be used for purposes such as:

  • improving gameplay and performance
  • fixing crashes or bugs
  • understanding feature quality and game stability
  • maintaining the security and integrity of the Game

5. Sharing of Information

MindOops Studio does not sell personal information from the current build of Paper Escape.

If a future version adds third-party services that process technical data on our behalf, such services will be disclosed in an updated Privacy Policy.

6. Children’s Privacy

Paper Escape is intended for a general audience. MindOops Studio does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the current build of the Game.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to MindOops Studio through the Game, please contact us so we can review the situation.

7. Data Retention

Since the current build does not intentionally collect personal information inside the Game, MindOops Studio does not maintain a player database containing personal account records for Paper Escape.

8. Security

MindOops Studio aims to take reasonable steps to protect the Game and any information associated with its operation. However, no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

MindOops Studio may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the Game evolves. If material changes are made, the updated policy will be posted with a new effective date.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact MindOops Studio at: mindsoops@gmail.com
